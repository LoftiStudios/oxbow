# Copyright Assignment — template

**Unexecuted template. Not legal advice.** Fill the two bracketed fields, export
to PDF, sign, and file it with the LLC's records. No addresses appear anywhere
in this document by design — parties to an assignment need to be identifiable,
not located.

**Blanks to fill:** the Effective Date, and the LLC's state of formation (used
twice — formation and governing law; normally the same state).

**On consideration:** this uses nominal consideration, which is valid and needs
nothing from your accountant. If they would rather treat the transfer as a
capital contribution, that is a bookkeeping characterisation they can apply
afterwards — the conveyance is effective either way.

**Sole authorship confirmed:** `git log` shows 93 commits, all authored by
Barclay Loftus, so there is one Assignor and no spousal signature block.

---

## COPYRIGHT ASSIGNMENT

Effective as of **[EFFECTIVE DATE]** (the "Effective Date").

**Assignor:** Barclay Loftus, an individual.
**Assignee:** Lofti Studios LLC, a **[STATE]** limited liability company.

**1. Assignment.** In consideration of One Dollar ($1.00) and other good and
valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is acknowledged,
Assignor hereby irrevocably assigns to Assignee all of Assignor's worldwide
right, title, and interest — including all copyrights, and all moral rights to
the extent they are assignable — in and to Assignor's original contributions to
the software project known as **Oxbow**, together with its source code,
documentation, build scripts, and artwork, and the goodwill associated with the
name "Oxbow" as used in connection with it (the "Work").

**2. Future contributions.** Assignor hereby assigns to Assignee, automatically
and immediately upon creation and without any further act, instrument, or
consideration, all such right, title, and interest in each contribution to the
Work that Assignor creates after the Effective Date.

**3. What is not assigned.** This assignment reaches only Assignor's own
contributions. It conveys nothing in (a) third-party components distributed with
the Work — including TwitchDownloaderCLI, FFmpeg, the .NET runtime, and any
bundled fonts — each of which remains its owner's and is used under its own
licence; or (b) contributions authored by anyone other than Assignor, which
remain their authors'.

**4. Existing licences preserved.** This assignment is subject to, and does not
revoke or limit, the MIT License previously granted to the public in the Work.

**5. Further assurances.** Assignor will execute such further documents as
Assignee reasonably requests in order to perfect or record this assignment.

**6. General.** This assignment is governed by the laws of the State of
**[STATE]**, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. It may be
executed in counterparts. An electronic signature has the same effect as an
original, and the parties intend it to satisfy the writing and signature
requirements of 17 U.S.C. § 204(a).
